Beauty by Manali – Beauty & Wellness App

Figma, FigJam, Photoshop, Canva, HTML5/CSS3 • 2024 – 2025

📌 Overview & Challenge

Modern users experience substantial decision fatigue when selecting specialized skincare and wellness products. The goal of Beauty by Manali was to design a soothing, accessible, and intuitive mobile app that empowers users through a guided skin-type quiz, personalized recommendations, and a spa-like minimalist aesthetic.

✨ Key Design Contributions

  • 50+ High-Fidelity Screens: Onboarding, quiz flow, product cards, dynamic cart drawer, wishlist, and frictionless checkout.
  • Design System: Curated botanical color palette with WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility ratios, auto-layout tokens, typography hierarchy, and micro-interaction states.
  • FigJam User Research: User empathy mapping and personas to streamline navigation from 5 clicks to 2 clicks.
